Musicality is the heart of capoeira. Without the bateria — berimbau leading, pandeiro marking and atabaque sustaining — the game loses its soul.

In our roda, every student learns not only ginga and esquiva, but also to sing ladainhas and respond to corridos. Music is not decoration: it dictates the rhythm of the game.

## The bateria instruments

The **berimbau** is the master of the roda. With its toques — Angola, São Bento Grande, Banguela — it defines whether the game will be slow and ritualistic or fast and explosive.
