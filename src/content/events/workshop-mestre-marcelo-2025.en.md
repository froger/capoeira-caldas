After 2 years on tour, travelling through Asia, Australia and Brazil, Mestre Marcelo Angola will be back in Europe!

Don't miss this enriching opportunity for your Capoeira and cultural creativity.

On 12 April from 12–4pm we will have the honour and pleasure of spending time with the Mestre in a workshop at the Angoleiros do Mar school in Caldas da Rainha.

Location: Sociedade Columbófila Caldense, Caldas da Rainha

Mestre Marcelo Angola founded the Angoleiros do Mar group in 1999 on the island of Itaparica, Bahia.
Thanks to his dedication and passion for Capoeira, the Mestre now has many capoeira schools around the world and has opened doors for many capoeiristas.

Participation fee: €40.
